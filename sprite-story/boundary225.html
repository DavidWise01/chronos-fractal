COST OF A CHROME CYCLE

What is the mileage?

The cell accounts for work rather than pretending all circulation is free. This example assigns 1.00 ledger unit per permutation and 0.25 units per observation. These are user-adjustable simulation conventions, not joules.

The four-wheel permutation has exact closure after four moves, but the energy ledger increases. Return to the same position does not mean zero work was performed.

CHRONOS assigns one arcsecond tick to light traveling 0.01 m in vacuum, ~33.3564 ps. Angle is a label; physical spatial distance requires a defined radius.
